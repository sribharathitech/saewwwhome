About Sri Bharathi

One corporate identity. Three specialist businesses.

Sri Bharathi is the corporate home for businesses serving industrial filtration, enterprise technology and chemical manufacturing needs. Each specialist website carries its own detailed products or services; this site provides the common company context.

FTC

A group built around specialist work

The parent organisation connects three distinct fields without pretending they are interchangeable. Filtration focuses on process media and application fit. Technology focuses on software engineering, modernisation and applied AI. Chemicals focuses on customer-defined manufacturing and blending services.

This structure helps a customer reach the relevant technical team while giving partners, search engines and prospective collaborators a clear view of the wider Sri Bharathi organisation.
