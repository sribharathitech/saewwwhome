Quality Approach at Sri Bharathi

Evidence against a defined requirement

Quality claims are only useful when the requirement, method and acceptance criteria are clear. Sri Bharathi’s corporate approach is to route each enquiry to the specialist business that can assess and document the relevant work.

FTC
01

Define

Capture the application, operating conditions, specification, responsibilities and result expected.

02

Verify

Identify the parameters, trials, checks or software quality activities relevant to the intended use.

03

Document

Agree the records and acceptance information that make a decision traceable and useful.

Filtration quality context

The filtration business describes monitored raw materials, automated pad production and routine checks including filtration efficiency, porosity, absorbency, filtration speed and bursting factor. The applicable criteria depend on the grade and intended use.
